2. What data is used

  • Telegram sign-in: Telegram ID, name and available profile details supplied by Telegram to verify sign-in. Your phone number is supplied separately through authorised contact sharing or manual entry.
  • Profile: name and phone number; optionally, email, city and saved preferences. Optional fields may be left blank, edited or cleared in your profile.

  • Service: booking history and statuses, enquiries and trip-related messages, ratings and reviews, referrals, promo codes, rewards and relevant administrative records.
  • Payments and accounting: selected payment method, amount, currency, payment and refund identifiers and statuses, and information needed for financial documents when the corresponding features are used.
  • Technical information: connection and request data, including IP address, request time, requested resource, browser and device details to the extent received by the server and resource providers.

Ordinary registration does not require passport copies or a passenger's personal identification code. Do not put card details, passwords, documents, health information, political views or other sensitive information unnecessary for organising a trip into comments.

3. Purposes and legal grounds

  • Registration, requested estimates, communication and providing a trip: entering into and performing a contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b). This also covers data necessary for optional profile and rewards features you choose to use.
  • Mandatory accounting and tax records: compliance with legal obligations, Article 6(1)(c).
  • Security, preventing abuse and duplicate requests, handling enquiries and protecting rights: legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f). The service's interests are safe service provision and protection against fraud and unfounded claims; only proportionate information is processed. You may object to this processing.
  • Obtaining device location: your voluntary consent, Article 6(1)(a). Refusal does not prevent manual address entry.

Without your name, phone number and required trip details, a booking cannot be arranged and fulfilled. Optional data is not a condition of travel. Reading this policy does not constitute consent to any processing or advertising. Advertising and non-essential tracking, if introduced, require an appropriate separate legal basis and a separate user choice.

The estimated price is calculated automatically from the route and selected options, then confirmed by the driver. You can contact the provider about the calculation. This estimate alone is not an automated refusal of service or a final decision producing legal effects.

4. Maps and location

You can enter an address, choose a map point or request device location. Permission can be withdrawn in Telegram, browser or device settings. Confirmed coordinates are used by OpenRouteService to identify the address and calculate the route. Points included in a request may be saved with the booking.

The map uses Leaflet and OpenStreetMap. Map requests give the map provider connection details and the area of requested map tiles. Loading external libraries also connects to their providers. Choosing a point is not intended to continuously track a passenger in the background.

7. How retention is determined

Retention depends on the purpose and record type; one ten-year period does not apply to all data:

  • Profile and optional preferences: while needed to use the service; on profile closure or a deletion request, we assess what may be deleted and what is still necessary for an active booking or by law.
  • Requests and trip history: during calculation, agreement, performance, payment and related enquiries. Further retention requires a specific need for mandatory records or protecting claims within an applicable period.
  • Financial documents: for the mandatory period under Lithuanian law for that document type. Keeping an invoice does not mean all comments and detailed coordinates must be kept.
  • Enquiries and disputes: until resolved and, where necessary, for the applicable period for bringing or defending related claims.
  • Technical logs and backups: within the necessary security and recovery cycle, taking provider settings into account. Deletion requests take temporary backups and their replacement cycle into account.

When the applicable basis ends, data must be deleted or reliably anonymised. This policy does not mean the app already automatically deletes records on a timer. Contact the email above for the retention of a specific record or a deletion request.

8. Cookies and browser storage

The app uses technical Telegram and browser mechanisms. sessionStorage holds a booking request identifier to prevent duplicate submissions; it is not an advertising profile. Storage behaviour also depends on Telegram and your browser.

Your chosen interface language is stored in the pd_lang cookie for up to 180 days and in localStorage until cleared. When switching languages, an unfinished form may be kept temporarily in this tab's sessionStorage and restored without being submitted. This temporary copy expires after five minutes and is removed when restored.

No separate advertising or analytics system is connected in the inspected PRIVATE DRIVE code. This does not remove technical processing by external platforms when loaded. Non-essential cookies, analytics or tracking added later will be used only after appropriate consent, with the ability to refuse and withdraw it. Browser settings let you clear cookies and local data; doing so may affect the app.

13. Clarifications: trips, payments and other passengers

If someone else makes a booking, the passenger's name, contact and necessary trip details come from the booker. Arranging and safely performing that trip relies on legitimate interests under GDPR Article 6(1)(f), or Article 6(1)(b) where the passenger is directly party to the contract; accounting obligations have a separate basis. The source and privacy information are supplied within Article 14 deadlines, normally on first contact and no later than one month unless a legal exception applies. You may ask about the source and object to legitimate-interest processing.

For accessibility arrangements, specify the assistance required, wheelchair space or child restraint; a diagnosis is not required. If processing health data is necessary, a separate Article 9 condition must be established beforehand; accepting trip terms is not that condition. Children's data is limited to what safe transport requires; marketing using children's data requires a separate lawful basis.

Bank transfers involve necessary payer details, reference, amount, date and receipt confirmation; refunds involve the required account information and refund confirmation. Crypto payments involve network, address and transaction identifier. Public blockchain records may persist independently of PRIVATE DRIVE; deleting a profile does not erase the blockchain. Banks and payment institutions may act as independent controllers under their own legal duties. Bank passwords and CVVs are not authorised disclosures.

Trip features record arrival, start and completion, actions and timestamps, acceptance of the waiting tariff, proposed extras and acceptance or rejection, related amounts and statuses. Purposes are contract performance and justified protection of claims. A recorded action is not conclusive evidence and may be challenged. This policy does not mean that every agreement is already recorded automatically. Profile history may retain previous and new values and change times; clearing a current field does not automatically clear its history. The same purpose and retention limits apply to these records; no default indefinite retention is established.

The passenger may see a location supplied by the assigned driver for pickup. This differs from obtaining the passenger's phone location at their choice. Trip-location disclosure stops on completion, cancellation or reassignment; this alone does not immediately erase the last database record. Further retention requires a necessary purpose under section 7. Continuous accuracy and signal availability are not guaranteed.

Access is limited to arranging the particular trip, support and legal duties. Sharing with another carrier requires establishing its role and legal basis; naming a recipient does not replace necessary safeguards and agreements. Security breaches follow GDPR Articles 33–34: where required, notify the authority without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of awareness; also notify affected individuals without undue delay where high risk requires it.
