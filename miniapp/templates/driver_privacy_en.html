Driver data privacy

26.10.2026 · PD-DRIVER-PRIVACY-2026-10-26

The controller is Yauheni Ivantsou, carrying on individual activity in Lithuania, Vilnius, Lietuva. Contact: eurobaltictransfer@gmail.com. PRIVATE DRIVE / Euro Baltic Transfer is the service name.

A driver application uses your Telegram ID, full name, phone number, city of operation, car make and model, year, registration number and passenger-seat count. Simplified registration applies during testing: only photos of both sides of the driving licence are required. Uploads for LTSA, individual activity, insurance, vehicle registration and personal photos are temporarily disabled. Previously provided documents remain in the protected application. Photos are used for application review; neither documents nor personal photos are published automatically. Only the applicant and authorised administrators can access them. EXIF metadata is removed during processing; images are stored with protected application data.

Data is used to review your application, contact you and prepare possible cooperation at your request: GDPR Article 6(1)(b). Limited administrator-action records serve security and protection of rights under legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f). Article 6(1)(c) applies where a legal obligation arises. Registration does not guarantee admission, assigned trips or income.

An authorised administrator reviews the application. Decisions and the review log are available only in the protected admin cabinet. Driver registration alone does not provide access to other people’s bookings or passenger data. Applications are not published in open channels.

The app uses Telegram and PythonAnywhere hosting. Their technical processing and possible transfers outside the EEA are described in the general privacy policy. We do not promise exclusive EU storage. Ask by email about the applicable basis and safeguards for a specific transfer.

Applications are kept during review and necessary contact about joining. Further retention requires continuing cooperation, a legal obligation or a need to protect claims within an applicable period. This notice does not mean automatic timed deletion is implemented. Ask by email about a particular record’s retention or deletion.

Where the GDPR provides, you may request access and a copy, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent prospectively where consent was the basis. Email eurobaltictransfer@gmail.com. We normally respond within one month; a permissible two-month extension is explained during the first month. Proportionate information is used to verify identity.

You may complain to Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija (VDAI), L. Sapiegos g. 17, LT-10312 Vilnius, ada@ada.lt, vdai.lrv.lt, or another competent GDPR authority. Contacting us first is not a condition of complaint. Cookies, language choice and connection security are explained in the general privacy policy.
