2. Requests and confirmation

  1. Provide the addresses, date, time, name, phone number, passenger count, luggage and any additional services. Times are in the Vilnius time zone unless another time zone is expressly agreed.
  2. Check the details and submit a preliminary request. A request number confirms receipt, but does not guarantee that a car will arrive.
  3. Wait for the driver's response. Before final agreement, the provider, route, date and time, vehicle or agreed vehicle characteristics, total price, payment method and applicable cancellation terms must be clear.
  4. The trip is agreed when both the provider and passenger expressly confirm these essential terms. A preliminary estimate or an app status change alone does not establish the passenger's agreement to a new price.

If the date, address or contact details are wrong, notify the provider before the trip. If you have not received confirmation, contact the provider; do not rely solely on having submitted a request.

3. Price, extras and changes

The estimate in euros depends on the route and selected options. The final total, applicable taxes and agreed extra costs must be confirmed before a paid booking.

  • The “With a pet” option currently adds 10% to the base estimated price. Check the updated total before submitting. Pet transport arrangements are agreed in advance; mandatory rights of passengers with assistance dogs remain unaffected.
  • An extra stop, waiting, a name-sign greeting, tolls or parking do not authorise arbitrary charges. If charged separately, the price or calculation method must be disclosed and agreed in advance.
  • If the route or other terms change, the provider explains the reason and the new amount. A price increase and additional payment require the passenger's agreement; an earlier request does not authorise unlimited subsequent charges.
  • Travel time and the displayed route are approximate. Traffic, weather and road restrictions may affect the trip, but do not automatically establish agreement to a higher price.

Before transferring funds, separately agree the euro price, exact amount, currency, network, address, fees and refund arrangements. Do not send funds to an unconfirmed address or over an unconfirmed network.

Do not send card details in messages or booking comments. These terms do not authorise additional charges without the required confirmation.

For airport pickup, agree the flight, meeting point and waiting terms in advance. For international travel, passengers ensure they have valid travel documents and meet entry requirements; ordinary registration does not require sending document copies.

Objective obstacles or safety threats may require a trip to be changed or ended. Where possible, the provider explains the reason and agrees the next steps. These terms do not exclude liability that cannot legally be limited or remove mandatory consumer rights.

10. Waiting, no-shows and extra costs

The current waiting feature allows 5 free minutes after the driver's recorded arrival at the agreed pickup point, then EUR 0.25 for each started minute. Charged time starts no earlier than both the passenger's separate acceptance of this tariff and the end of the free period; it ends when the trip starts. Arrival time, location and tariff must be communicated before acceptance. Incorrect arrival records may be challenged; waiting caused by the provider is not charged to the passenger. Individual tariff changes require prior agreement and must be reflected in the calculation.

A no-show is not established merely by failure to answer in the app. Correct arrival location and time, the agreed waiting period and contact attempts must be considered. Cancellation and no-show charges require a lawful, proportionate basis disclosed in advance. No automatic fine or unconditional forfeiture of the whole advance is introduced. Claims take account of services actually supplied, justified expenses, costs saved and reasonable mitigation; the passenger receives a calculation and may challenge it.

12. Conduct, damage, luggage and liability

Passengers provide accurate information, attend the agreed pickup at the agreed time, follow safety rules, do not smoke and do not carry dangerous or prohibited items. Child seats, oversized luggage and special arrangements must be agreed in advance. The driver may refuse to start or safely end a trip for an objective safety threat, unlawful conduct or inability to comply with mandatory rules; any settlement considers the cause, service supplied and law. Disability or lawful use of an assistance dog alone does not justify refusal or arbitrary surcharges.

Damage caused by the passenger's fault or necessary exceptional cleaning must be supported by the circumstances, photographs without unnecessary personal information and reasonable cost evidence. Ordinary wear is not charged. No arbitrary fixed fine, automatic debit or double recovery is established; objections remain possible. Report lost items with the booking number; return and any delivery are agreed separately. These provisions do not exclude statutory luggage liability.

Estimated travel time does not guarantee arrival for a flight; passengers should allow a reasonable margin. Liability for a provider's proven breach follows applicable law. Traffic, breakdowns and a contractor's mistakes do not automatically constitute force majeure. Exceptional obstacles require notice and reasonable mitigation; payment for an unprovided service is not automatically forfeited. Liability for death or personal injury, intentional misconduct, gross negligence and other non-excludable liability is preserved, as are mandatory passenger rights and access to courts.

Consumer complaints are handled free of charge, with a reasoned response and relevant supporting evidence within 14 calendar days unless a specific law requires otherwise.
